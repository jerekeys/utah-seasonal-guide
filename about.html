What the ratings mean

Scare level describes intensity, from gentle (1) to extreme (5). Festive Factor describes the amount of seasonal atmosphere, from a light touch (1) to an immersive celebration (5). Activity level runs from relaxed (1) to demanding (5). These are editorial guides to the advertised experience, not customer reviews or judgments about a tradition.

Access and accommodations

Find organizer information about wheelchair access, adaptive appointments and other accommodations on event pages when it is available. An event without access information may still offer the support you need. Ask the organizer about your requirements before booking.

If something on this website prevents you from using it, email Jere with the page and what happened.

Before you go

Check the event page for tickets, weather closures, cancellations and the latest hours. Prices can vary by date and may have fees.
