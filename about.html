Utah Seasonal Guide

About this guide

A public-facing layer on top of a deliberately over-researched Utah Halloween and fall spreadsheet.

What’s included

Commercial haunts, farms and pumpkin patches, media-publicized residential displays, museums, libraries, concerts, theater, adult nightlife, queer events, ghost history, unusual seasonal experiences, markets, races, food events and road-trip-worthy fall spectacles.

Accessibility approach

The site is built with semantic headings, keyboard-visible focus states, skip navigation, large tap targets, descriptive labels, reduced-motion support, meaningful alternative text for event-specific photography, decorative empty-alt artwork, and a higher-contrast color system. Accessibility is an ongoing practice rather than a one-time certification; corrections are welcome through the submission form.

Images

When an organizer, venue, attraction or official promoter publishes a strong event-specific hero or promotional image, the guide may use a locally hosted copy to identify and illustrate that event. Source and visible credit information are retained when available. Category artwork is used when a suitable event-specific image is not available.

Before you go

Seasonal events change quickly. Prices, hours, weather closures, availability and sell-outs can change after this guide is updated. Confirm the official page before driving or buying tickets.

Watch-list entries

The Explore directory defaults to confirmed 2026 activities. Historical leads and items awaiting a strong current-year signal are available through the “Include watch / historical leads” filter.

Corrections

Know a missing event or spot something wrong? Send a submission or correction.
