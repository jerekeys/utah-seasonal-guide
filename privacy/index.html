Privacy

Effective October 6, 2026

Your saved events

Saved event IDs stay in your browser’s local storage or installed app. They are not sent to the guide’s editor. Your downloaded export file contains event IDs and titles, not your name or email. Browser storage and installed-app storage may be separate.

Forms and follow-up

When you submit a form, its contents are processed and stored by Netlify, our hosting provider, so the guide can review your suggestion or respond. We may keep the message in our correspondence records. Please do not send sensitive personal information. We do not publish your follow-up name or email in a listing, sell your contact details or add you to a mailing list through these forms.

Event details you suggest may be edited and published. Tell us if a detail should stay private. To request deletion of your submitted contact information, send a privacy request; we may need enough information to verify which message is yours. Records necessary for a legal obligation or an active dispute may need to be retained.

Hosting and offline storage

Netlify may process technical request information, such as IP address, browser information and access logs, to deliver and protect the site and handle form abuse. The guide does not currently add third-party advertising trackers or analytics scripts. Offline support caches public guide pages, event data and images on your device; it does not cache or queue form submissions.

Other websites

Organizer, ticketing, maps and calendar links lead to services with their own privacy practices. Nothing is sent to them by saving an event here. Opening a link or creating a calendar event is your choice.

Questions

Contact the guide about privacy, or read our terms and conditions.
