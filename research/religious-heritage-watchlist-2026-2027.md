## Additional tag-coverage targets to investigate
- Eid al-Fitr and Eid al-Adha: Utah mosques/Islamic centers and community festivals; verify moon-sighting-dependent schedules and public accessibility.
- Holi: Spanish Fork and other temple/community celebrations; verify 2027 dates and public attendance.
- Ramadan: public iftar opportunities, not ordinary private worship.
- Passover, Rosh Hashanah, Yom Kippur, Sukkot, Purim: prioritize open public community programming; do not assume services are tourist events.
- Vesak, Sikh Vaisakhi, Baisakhi, Gurpurab: consult local Buddhist and gurdwara organizers.
- Native American/Indigenous heritage and powwows: distinguish public invitation from community-only ceremonies.
- Hispanic/Latino heritage, Día de los Muertos, Mexican Independence, Puerto Rican and other distinct heritage celebrations.
- Italian, Filipino, Pacific Islander, Asian, Greek, Scottish, Swiss, and other heritage celebrations.
- Black History Month, Asian American/Pacific Islander Heritage Month, Indigenous Peoples' Day: find actual public event programming, not just observance dates.
- Orthodox Christmas and Easter, Catholic feast days, interfaith celebrations, and other traditions represented in the existing taxonomy.

## Research & publishing rules
1. Match the **existing** Google Sheet holiday/heritage tags first; report missing coverage by exact tag. Avoid conflating faiths, nationalities, or communities.
2. Status options: organizer confirmed; dated secondary source; recurring / date unannounced; prospect; canceled.
3. A holiday date by itself is NOT an event listing. No fabricated times, locations, prices, accessibility accommodations, or year rollover.
4. Respect whether a service/ceremony is genuinely open to the general public; avoid listing private worship or community-only events as attractions.
5. Capture official source URL, latest verified date, organizer, accessibility statement, pricing, imagery/rights, and next check date.
6. For dated events, draft concise visitor-facing descriptions without internal notes or promotional fluff. Do not publish without confirmation.
7. Image policy: only accurately representative, rights-documented, locally hosted photographs; never hotlink.
8. Look for source updates monthly, increasing to weekly 6–8 weeks before observance; don't claim automated monitoring is active unless a task is created.
