## Recommended launch pilot (editorial recommendation, not an established market rate)

- $35 for seven days or $99 for 30 days, one featured event card, with no more than two paid cards above search.
- Consider a $20/week introductory nonprofit/community rate without making paid access necessary for ordinary inclusion.
- Keep ordinary directory listings free. Do not sell ratings, accommodation flags or false claims of endorsement.
- Publish only after written agreement on placement, exact dates, price, creative rights, cancellations and make-good/refund terms.
- Sell a fixed-duration placement while traffic is unknown; do not promise impressions, clicks or sales. Measure actual performance before adopting CPM or raising rates.
- A future dashboard should distinguish served/viewed impressions from page loads. Current site does not add tracking or claim impression analytics.

The public About page invites inquiries and quotes rather than publishing this pilot as a binding rate card. No sponsor is currently booked or displayed.

Advertising disclosure basis: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/native-advertising-guide-businesses . The paid card and destination event page both disclose paid placement. This report is commercial planning; sponsorship contracts and tax/payment setup are separate work.
