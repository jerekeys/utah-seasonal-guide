# Year-round discovery scope — October 2026 through October 2027

Seasons, holidays, religious observances and heritage/community celebrations are separate dimensions. Events may carry multiple supported tags; being in October alone does not make an event Halloween, and LGBTQ+ community tagging alone does not make an event a Pride celebration. Fall harvest destinations with explicit haunted programming carry both Fall and Halloween.

The dropdown defaults to All and groups the full discovery roster. Empty buckets stay available with a clear no-announced-events message. The roster CSV is planning context, not confirmed event inventory. Public event dates, hours, ticket prices and calendar entries require organizer evidence. Religious dates may begin at sunset, depend on moon sighting or vary by tradition. Yom Kippur 2026 has passed; the next observance belongs to the October 2027 horizon.

Sources: Hebcal 5787 calendar; American River College interfaith planning calendar; Library of Congress commemorative observance guides. Month windows marked variable are intentionally not exact event dates.

Utah search leads: Utah Pride Center, Ogden Pride, Southern Utah Pride, Utah Black Chamber/community cultural organizations, state/city libraries, university cultural centers, Indigenous-led organizations, Jewish congregations/JCC, Islamic centers, Hindu temples, Sikh gurdwaras, Buddhist temples, Bahá’í communities and Pagan community calendars. Recurring organizer names are discovery leads until their next event is announced.

This is a broad initial roster, not a claim that every faith, culture or observance has been enumerated. Add specific buckets when community evidence justifies them. Do not classify private worship as a public attraction or assume visitors are invited.
