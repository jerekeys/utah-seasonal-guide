What the ratings mean

Scare level describes intensity, from gentle (1) to extreme (5). Festive Factor describes seasonal atmosphere, from a light touch (1) to an immersive celebration (5). Activity level runs from relaxed (1) to demanding (5). These are editorial guides to the advertised experience, rather than customer reviews or judgments about a tradition.

Access and accommodations

Event pages include organizer information about mobility, hearing, vision, sensory support and adaptive appointments when we can verify it. An event without access information may still offer the support you need. Ask the organizer about your requirements before booking.

If something on this website prevents you from using it, report an accessibility problem. Tell us which page you were using and what happened.

Independent event choices

We choose which events to include and may edit descriptions, tags, ratings and images to help visitors compare options. An ordinary listing is free; submitting an event does not guarantee publication.

Sponsored listings

Organizers and local businesses can ask about paid featured cards above the event search. Each paid placement is clearly labeled Sponsored advertisement. Sponsorship pays for visibility; it does not buy a rating or change the admission and accommodation information on an event page.

Before you go

Check the organizer’s page for tickets, weather closures, cancellations and current hours. Prices may vary by date, age and mandatory fees.
