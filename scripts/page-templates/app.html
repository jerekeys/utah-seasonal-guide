Keep the guide handy.

The same Utah guide, with its own home-screen icon and window.

Choose the instructions for your phone below.

iPhone or iPad

  1. Open this website in Safari. If you followed a link inside Instagram or another app, open it in Safari first.
  2. Open Safari’s Share menu and choose Add to Home Screen. On newer versions, you may need the More menu first.
  3. If shown, turn on Open as Web App, then tap Add.
  4. Open the Utah Guide icon on your home screen.

Android

  1. Open the guide in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install the guide above when it appears, or open Chrome’s menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Confirm, then open the Utah Guide icon.

What comes with it?

Holiday and date filters, event pages, saved events and calendar links work in the app window. Once you have opened the guide online, you can browse cached listings offline. Photos and event pages you have not opened may need a connection. Ticket purchases and sending forms always need internet access.

Save this guide from the address you plan to keep using. A private development preview and the public website are separate installations with separate saved lists.

Keep your saved list

No account is needed. Export your saved events before installing, then import the file in the installed guide if your list is empty. Lists do not sync across devices.

Updates

Open the guide with an internet connection to get current event information. If an app update is available, a refresh button will appear. Offline information can be out of date.
